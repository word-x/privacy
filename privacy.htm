Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Word-X Studio (https://www.word-x.com) regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile applications and related services (collectively, the “Service”). It also explains your privacy rights and how applicable laws protect you.

Word-X Studio is committed to protecting your privacy. Our applications are designed to function fully offline and, in their current versions, do not collect personal data.

1. Definitions

Application refers to any mobile application developed by Word-X Studio that includes or references this Privacy Policy, including but not limited to TimyNote (https://timynote.word-x.com).

Personal Data means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.

Service refers to the Application and any related functionality provided by Word-X Studio.

We, Us, or Our refers to Word-X Studio.

2. Information Collection and Use

2.1 No Personal Data Collection

The Application can be used 100% offline and does not require user registration. Therefore, we do not collect, store, or process personal data, including but not limited to:

  • Name, address, or contact information
  • Email address or phone number
  • Photos, media files, or contacts
  • Location data
  • Identifiers such as IP address or device ID

2.2 Local Storage Permission

The Application may request permission to access local storage solely for the purpose of saving application-related data such as:

  • User-created lists
  • Vocabulary or test progress

This data remains on your device at all times. We do not access, read, upload, or transmit other files stored on your device.

3. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not retain personal data on our servers. All application data stored locally remains under your control and can be deleted by uninstalling the Application or clearing its data.

4. Data Transfer

The Application does not transmit personal data to Word-X Studio or any third party. If future versions introduce data collection features, appropriate security measures such as encryption will be applied, and this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Disclosure of Data

We do not sell, trade, or share personal data with third parties.

6. Data Security

Although no personal data is collected, we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the integrity of the Application and any locally stored data. If future updates involve personal data processing, industry-standard security practices will be implemented.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR or similar regulations). As the Application does not collect personal data, these rights generally do not require action. If data collection is introduced in the future, you will be informed in advance and provided with applicable choices.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us, and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.

If future changes materially affect how data is handled, we will provide additional notice within the Application or through appropriate channels before the changes take effect.
